How to Perform

  1. Set the category and force item above, then save.
  2. Write your prediction (the force item) on paper or in a sealed envelope.
  3. Ask the spectator to open /pick on their phone.
  4. They see a numbered list of 100 items. Let them browse freely.
  5. Ask them to name any number from 1 to 100.
  6. Take their phone. Triple-tap the header to open the secret pad.
  7. Enter their number on the pad and tap ✓ to confirm.
  8. The force item silently moves to that position. The page scrolls there.
  9. Show them: "Number 42 is Paris!" Then reveal your prediction.
